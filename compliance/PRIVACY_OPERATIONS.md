# ACTS privacy operations — 4.0.1 / 27 September 2026

Owner: Nicholas McIntyre. Public contact: actsauctioneertraining@gmail.com.

- Review tester and support records periodically and at the end of a testing round. Retain only while needed for testing, requests, security, disputes or legal obligations; record the reason for exceptions. No automatic retention deadline is promised by the policy.
- On withdrawal/deletion, verify the requester using the registration email or reasonable evidence. Do not request a store password or full payment-card number.
- Delete the matching registration using the private registrations dashboard. Its authenticated DELETE endpoint checks both record ID and email. This code change does not delete any existing registration automatically.
- Separately remove exported CSV/email-list copies under ACTS control, and the corresponding Google Play tester-list entry. The website cannot delete those external copies automatically.
- For app data: explain local deletion, backups and exported PDFs. There is no ACTS cloud training database to erase remotely. A local reset does not cancel store billing.
- For identifiable support/RevenueCat records, assess the verified request and use the provider's deletion tools where appropriate, preserving records required by law. Removing a RevenueCat record can affect access/identification; do not cancel or refund a purchase merely because data deletion was requested.
- Confirm what was removed, anything retained and why, and the separate Apple/Google subscription cancellation steps. Escalate unresolved privacy complaints appropriately.
- Restrict the tester administration key to authorised staff, rotate if disclosed, never include it in URLs, and protect downloaded lists. Tester consent is for testing, not general marketing.
